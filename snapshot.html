Snapshot of a Control Agenda

Undergraduate thesis, 2025

TouchDesigner, Unity, MediaPipe, OSC

You are aware of your behaviors, preferences, and all the characteristics that make up the being you call "yourself". None of these traits are static, nor are they immune to interpretation by someone else. What do you become when your persona is run through varying lenses of power?

Snapshot of a Control Agenda is a brief game that places the player under the judgement of several careful observers as a means of exposing the irrational performance of gender, and gender's subjugation by powers that seek to exploit it.

Let the unassuming pieces of your character be scrutinized by the Administrator, reconstructed by the Garden Patch, and thrown away by a stranger. The schemes of today's most influential authorities are too grand to see all at once; Snapshot offers a glimpse into the pathology behind their actions unto us.

Technical process

Snapshot runs between TouchDesigner as the tracking input and mask-maker, and Unity as the game engine and event driver. A webcam picks up the player's face using TouchDesigner's MediaPipe plugin.

The player self-identifies as male, female, or "other", and is internally assigned a value of 0 (male), 1 (female), or 0.5 (other). The player then answers simple preferential questions devised from traits traditionally associated with binary gender roles. Each option is assigned "0" for "masculine", or "1" for "feminine". If the average of the player's choices does not closely match the number assigned to them at the beginning, they do not pass the screening.

Each response to a question creates a new feature on the base mesh mask, forming an absurd representation of your habits and values through the most absolute lens of the "gender binary".

Narratively, the screening represents a compromise between two entities: the accelerationist tech lobby and the present U.S. Administration. The program, Garden Patch, is a fictional tycoon-style simulation of the theoretical government system called the Network State. It invites the player to sign up for its Beta release, but it is the Administrator, a mandated addition to the program, that demands a gendered assignment before the player may proceed.

A third entity, a malicious worm called RAYMU, was developed to intercept the player after the screening to provide context as the third cultural force representing a crypto-adjacent extremist community. The character was scrapped due to time constraints.
